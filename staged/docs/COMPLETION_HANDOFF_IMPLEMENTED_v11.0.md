# AGVG Lab Tools v11.0 - Concurrent-validation release

## Operating model

Student use is open. Experimental is a claim boundary, not a disabled button.
Every stamped output declares one Part VIII level: computational regression,
technical validation, biological validation, or publication use. Publication
certification is the only validation-rung refusal. Correctness refusals remain:
no worm-level inference in population mode, no stimulus certification from an
unidentifiable design, and no forced positive outcomes.

## Validation evidence

Mandatory review records inspected agreements and disagreements, separated by
tool, condition, and version. The denominator is all reviewed proposals.
Failure reports remain local-first and reproducible. Config 2 stores a tested
envelope rather than a free-floating boolean when technical validation passes.
Outside-envelope recordings remain runnable but carry warnings and the lower
stamp.

## Power analysis

The Utilities workbench plans independent plate replication for linear
plate-summary tests, Rayleigh tests, and two-sample Watson U2 circular
comparisons. Retrospective mode estimates plate variance by assay and strain
from stamped beta streams. Mixed versions or validation levels are separated
and flagged; fewer than six plates is provisional and widens the planned n.

## Existing assay completion

Chemotaxis/avoidance, linear/radial thermotaxis, longitudinal healthspan,
paralysis pharmacology, mechanosensation/habituation, and graded-resistance
burrowing remain the shared-engine implementations already present in v10.4.
They ship runnable and computational-regression stamped. Flat/no-event outcomes,
censoring, plate-first inference, and human review remain intact.

## T8 completion

Tier 2 per-worm vectorial magnetotaxis runs at computational-regression level.
Config 2 crowded-plate evidence controls technical promotion and publication
certification. The output includes local start-field magnitude/gradient and
descriptive dwell and departure-latency regressions. Across-plate aggregation
compares pulsed and unpulsed plates for latency and angular shifts.

## Deferred

Single-channel GCaMP remains outside this handoff. When resumed, its
Capability-Gate feasibility pass precedes extraction.
