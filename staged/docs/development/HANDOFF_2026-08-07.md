## 2. The scientific claim

DMD does not affect all muscles equally. Comparisons across separate animals
cannot resolve whether tissues decline together or independently, because
biological variation is confounded with tissue identity. The claim needs
**within-individual covariance** across muscle groups.

Three muscle groups, three readouts:

| tissue | functional readout | structural readout |
|---|---|---|
| body wall (striated) | crawling kinematics | confocal |
| pharyngeal (cardiac-like) | pumping rate | confocal |
| enteric | defecation cycle | none |

Two facts make it tractable, both from Andrés:

1. A single confocal stack of a head contains **both** body wall and
   pharyngeal muscle. Two tissues, one animal, one acquisition.
2. A single behavioural recording often contains **more than one** readout.
   A defecation movie contains bouts of crawling and bouts of pumping.

Behaviour and confocal are **separate preparations**. No animal has both.
Each leg is internally within-individual; they are between-individual with
respect to each other. Do not write anything implying otherwise.

## 3. The measurement model

**The unit is the bout, not the recording.** A recording yields some
quantity of usable material per readout, differing by readout within the
same file. Output is a per-readout budget: usable seconds, bout count,
and unusable time split by cause.

**Unusable time must be attributed.** Not performing is biology and is data.
Not visible is acquisition failure and is missing data. Conflating them lets
poor recordings masquerade as low pump rates. Claude Code called this the
sharpest thing in the plan.

**Reference pools.** Plentiful single-readout recordings establish per-strain
baselines. Scarcer multi-readout recordings carry the covariance, scored
relative to strain reference. No eligible recording is wasted. This was
Andrés's idea and it is what makes the archive coherent.

**Validation, two independent references, both held out:**
- Legacy human-scored datasets (pumping, defecation, swimming frequency).
  Never develop against these. Once tuned, agreement stops being evidence.
- Published pezo-1 pumping phenotype (Hughes et al. 2022, G3 12(3):jkab429).
  Positive control in pharyngeal channel, negative in crawling. Note the
  phenotype is **conditional on food density**, not a flat rate offset, so
  it needs the food condition of each recording known.

## 5. Key insights that came from Andrés, not from analysis

These unstuck problems that algorithm work had failed on. Both are the
pattern worth noticing: domain knowledge beat iteration.

**"OP50 is smooth, worms are textured."** Five intensity-based segmentation
rules all segmented the shadow, not the animal, and all produced
reasonable-looking numbers. The texture rule works. Substrate gate uses
DoG σ 6 to 24 (lawn wrinkles); tip extension uses σ 1 to 3 (worm texture).
Never unify them: extending on the substrate band walks off the animal at
the tail, where evidence is thinnest, and looks successful.

**"What I focus on is the change in direction."** Andrés's own perceptual
mechanism for pumping, pBoc and swimming is **direction reversal**, not
contraction shape. It comes down to one frame. This explains why 15 fps
works for his eyes, unifies the three readouts, and is more robust to low
contrast than convergence detection. **The head-to-head test against
convergence is specified and not yet run.** Prediction: reversal holds to a
lower frame rate and then fails sharply rather than degrading gracefully.
If it degrades gracefully instead, the model is wrong.

A meta-observation Andrés made, worth carrying: building the detector forced
him to articulate expertise that had been tacit for twenty years, including
how he teaches students to see it. Neither he nor they had ever stated the
criterion. This belongs in the October Discourse update.

## 6. Hard-won lessons, all from real failures this session

1. **A backlog item names a symptom, not a location.** Most items turned out
   to be reachability failures, not missing code.
2. **When a measurement is withdrawn, walk forward through what was built on
   it.** This bit twice, and both times the orphaned number appeared to
   confirm an expectation, which is what let it survive.
3. **Two numbers agreeing is evidence only if the spread of each is smaller
   than the gap between them.** This is the general form of all three
   coincidences that fooled us.
4. **Confirm what was segmented before using anything derived from it.**
5. **A plateau is evidence a feature is right; a threshold that happens to
   work is not.**
6. **"Largest component" is an unsafe selector.** It returned a fragment of
   a different animal, which passes every check a debris blob would fail.
7. **Measured properties, not declared ones.** Files declared 16-bit held 8
   bits left-shifted. A four-digit-year regex read 91 of 5,206 filenames;
   this lab uses seven date conventions and YYMMDD dominates.
8. **A single frame cannot estimate a directional property.** One frame gave
   azimuth 146° at R=0.56; twenty-five gave 94° at 0.86. Posture
   contaminates.
9. **Record failed approaches next to the constants**, especially plausible
   ones. The second azimuth attempt looked like a fix for the first.

## 9. The architectural finding

Six reachability failures in one tool. Claude Code's diagnosis, which is
correct and worth preserving:

**A. Launch-by-subprocess with no shared state.** Every cross-module call is
`subprocess.Popen([python, script, path])`, so only what fits in argv can
cross, and each call site reinvents which fragment to send. The GCaMP
handoff sent the recording and forgot the range; the `g` key sent a derived
directory and forgot both. Fix: one serialisable analysis context passed as
`--context file.json`.

**B. A capability with no route from the screen that needs it.** Contrast
dials existed in two other tools and were reachable from neither tracker
screen. Not architectural. Fix: an audit producing a list.

**And a third, found late:** `app/lab_hub.py:877` can relaunch the Hub from
a published tree if an update is accepted mid-session, while the version
string stays at 11.137. So what is running can change underneath a session
and neither Andrés nor a student can tell. This belongs in the publishing
investigation as a first-class item.
